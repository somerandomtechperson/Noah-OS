THE PROJECT

Linux, with a personal touch.

NoahOS is a custom Linux distribution built around Arch Linux and KDE Plasma, focused on creating a clean, modern, and cohesive desktop experience. I wanted an OS that felt like home, something you know and love, even if you've never tried it before. MacOS came to my mind, and I decided to get to work. I used Arch Linux as a base for NoahOS, and built everything around it. I first installed KDE Plasma, as this would be an obvious choice becuase of its flexibility and minimal RAM and CPU usage. I wanted something that I could use everyday, and thats what NoahOS is.

OUR APPROACH

Built with intention.

NoahOS brings together the flexibility of Arch Linux and the power of KDE Plasma in a thoughtfully customized desktop environment.

From its visual identity to its desktop layout, NoahOS aims to make the Linux desktop feel consistent, approachable, and enjoyable to use.

THE FOUNDATION

Powered by great open-source software.

Arch Linux

A flexible Linux distribution that provides the solid foundation for NoahOS.

KDE Plasma

A powerful and customizable desktop environment that brings the NoahOS experience to life.

Archiso

The toolset used to build the bootable NoahOS live environment.

CURRENT STATUS

A project in development.

NoahOS is actively being developed. Its current release provides a bootable live environment that can be explored without installing it to a computer.

Features, documentation, and the overall experience may continue to evolve as the project grows.
